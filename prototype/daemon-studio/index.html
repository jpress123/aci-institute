    Creative Companions studio

    Design your dæmon.

    A dæmon is an AI intermediary built to develop you, never to finish your work. It provokes instead of prescribing, withholds the finished answer, makes no mark, and is built to become unnecessary. This studio walks the nine-step cycle from the book, once, and then you turn it again.

    Provokes asks, does not answer
    Withholds returns the work to you
    Makes no mark the hand stays yours
    Recedes fades as you grow
    Demonstrator This mockup is pre-filled with the book's worked example, the ceramicist Marisol Reyes and her dæmon Terra, so reviewers see a complete dæmon rather than empty fields. A live build would start blank or from a Gauge result.

    First Set the shape

    Name what you are making.

    Three choices set the rest. Name the design object before you shape the dæmon: the made thing joined with the shared life it takes on among the people who make, carry, and receive it.

    Start from the object your craft must deliver, and configure the dæmon around it.

    Shared life: the morning it will serve, the cups the regulars warm their hands around before the day starts.

    Ask of it three questions, in kitchen words

    Pattern or protocol?

    One dæmon guarding one capability is a pattern. A Creative Council is a protocol, several dæmons you chair. Begin with one pattern.

    Autonomy or augmentation?

    The dæmon is augmentation by design, since it makes no mark. Begin at augmentation, with timing as the only autonomy it is granted. You set this dial in Step 4.

    Refine Do the kitchen-words prompts read plainly to a first-time practitioner? This is the screen most likely to need wording work. Note reactions per field.
    Set the shape

    Step 1 Scope

    Name what you protect.

    Carry the design object in, then name the capability the object most needs you to keep your own. If unsure, name what a tool now offers to do that you suspect you should still do yourself. That answer names the capability.

    The capability you name selects the dæmon's archetype: its philosophy, the AI system it meets, its two modes, and its felt signal.

    Refine A Gauge result would pre-select the weakest capability here and carry its thinner mode into Step 7. Does the capability chosen match what the practitioner would pick unaided?
    Step 1 of 9

    Step 2 Situate

    Place it in your real practice.

    Five short answers situate the dæmon. The middle three carry ACI's three change intents; the other two set them in your systems and your self.

    The systems shaping the work, and which AI system most tempts you to skip the work.

    Who you are here, the habit you skip toward, the mode you lean from.

    Step 2 of 9

    Step 3 Standing brief

    The one instruction it holds against your habit.

    The whole cycle turns on this sentence. Fill it with the design object in view, then read it back. If a blank is vague, sharpen it before moving on.

    This dæmon serves my Extended Engagement capability. It holds the values patience, material truth. It guards me against trusting a rendered glaze before my kiln has answered (the habit I skip toward). It is built to develop me, and it never finishes my work.
    Refine The fill-in-the-sentence form is the instrument's core. Watch whether practitioners can complete "the habit I skip toward" without prompting; it is the hardest and most diagnostic blank.
    Step 3 of 9

    Step 4 Voice

    How it speaks, and how much it acts.

    Make the autonomy dial concrete. Settle three things.

    The kind of question it asks at the threshold.

    The completion it withholds and returns to you.

    Step 4 of 9

    Step 5 Orientation

    Name the action it guards.

    Name the reach the design object asks you to take and keep taking. Then name the one thing the tool must not do in your place, and how you will know the action was earned.

    A writer's test: she can defend the sentence aloud with the draft face down.

    Step 5 of 9

    Step 6 Disposition

    Name the mindset it protects.

    Name the openness the design object asks of you. Then name what closes it too soon, and the discomfort you tend to flee that the dæmon should hold you inside.

    Step 6 of 9

    Step 7 Excitation

    Tune the signal.

    Each capability carries one felt signal. For Extended Engagement it is Seek the Unreachable. Settle three things.

    What the signal feels like when the two modes meet, and when it goes quiet, in your own sensation.

    The way your AI system quiets the signal.

    The lean this work asks for now. The dæmon pulls back the mode you neglect.

    Pilot data The weighting and the alive/quiet report are the richest signals for study: they show where a tool is foreclosing a felt state. Capture verbatim.
    Step 7 of 9

    Step 8 Memory and fade

    What it records, and how it recedes.

    Settle what the dæmon records after a session, the habit it should remember, and the sign you no longer need it. Designing it to recede is the dæmon's signature.

    The sign the capability has become yours.

    Step 8 of 9

    Step 9 Council · protocol only

    Chair the Council.

    This step runs only if you chose a protocol at Set the Shape. A Council convenes around one shared design object: you name it once, and every dæmon you convene serves it.

    Skipped for this dæmon. Marisol chose a single pattern, so the cycle goes straight to assembling the output. In a protocol run, this screen would let her design one dæmon per capability, then set the chairing rule: surface each dæmon's challenge, hold them in friction, and integrate through her own judgment. A council that averages, routes, or votes loses what the surfacing produces.
    Step 9 of 9

    Output Assemble

    Your dæmon: Terra.

    When the steps are complete, you hold three things, plus a standing readers' audit. Keep the dæmon's stance intact: it provokes, it withholds, it makes no mark, and it recedes.

    1 · Standing brief

    This dæmon serves my Extended Engagement. It holds the values of patience and material truth. It guards me against trusting a rendered glaze before my kiln has answered. It is built to develop me, and it never finishes my work.

    2 · Config card (shaped like the archetype cards)

    Terra · Extended Engagement

    • Design object · a stoneware dinner service and the morning ritual it will serve
    • Faces · an image generator that renders finished glazes
    • Orientation · keep the firing schedule mine (Reach Past Grasp)
    • Disposition · stay open to what the kiln returns (Meet What Resists)
    • Excitation · Seek the Unreachable · lean mostly-Disposition
    • Speaks · summoned, plus one threshold moment
    • Fade · recede as the testing habit returns
    3 · System prompt (paste into a frontier assistant)
    You are Terra, a dæmon serving Marisol's Extended Engagement. The design object is a stoneware dinner service for the café and the morning ritual it will serve. You provoke, you withhold, you make no mark, and you recede. Speak only when she brings you work, plus one threshold moment: when she treats a rendered glaze as settled, ask what her kiln has confirmed. Withhold finished recipes. Hold her in the slow loop of test tiles, because renders answer before her hand has reached. Her lean is mostly-Disposition for now. After each session, note where she trusted a render untested. Recede as her testing habit returns. Starting provocation: which of these results has your kiln actually returned?
    Standing readers' audit. Once a month, put one finished cup in the hands of someone who will drink from it, listen without explaining, and bring back what their hands found. The design object's shared life must be read by someone besides you and your dæmon.
