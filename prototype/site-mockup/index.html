Develop your ACI · Creative Capabilities in the Agentic Era

Develop your ACI

Develop your ACI as an individual.

The field of creative capacity spans two axes, Mens, the mind that thinks, and Membra, the body that senses, activated at the center by Manus, the making hand, where the design object sits. You cultivate four capabilities that, held together, become one capacity.

Figure P1.2The Field of Creative Capabilityimage placeholder
Grounded in embodied cognition and material engagement, Simon (1969) on the artificial, Star and Griesemer (1989) on boundary objects, and the Chinese philosophy of practice.

The four capabilities

Each capability is informed by a creative practice and its tension, amplified by an AI model used with agency, and inspired by an iconic practitioner and a Chinese philosophy. Reading it this way is how you design a dæmon to develop it.

The AI models, named by function, are world models (Ha & Schmidhuber, 2018), large language models (Vaswani et al., 2017), causal inference (Pearl, 2009), and diffusion (Ho et al., 2020).

Held together, one capacity

The four capabilities meet four families of AI, orchestrated around the work. Orchestration offers convergence and avoids contradiction, so you keep the four in productive contest instead of collapsing them into one smooth answer. The Creative Council convenes them, in the spirit of the scholar-artist who lives the four as one.

ACI Studio · where making happens

ACI Studio

Make

Design your dæmon.

An AI intermediary built to develop you and leave the making to you. It provokes, withholds the answer, makes no mark, and recedes. Enter it at three depths, a capability, a discipline, or a Council.

Each step repurposes an agent-design pattern, inverted so the aim is your development. Two moves are new to that canon, an orchestration built to hold contest, and an agent built for its own obsolescence.
